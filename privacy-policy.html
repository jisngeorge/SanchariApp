Sanchari App - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2027
Last updated: 1 November 2027

Sanchari is an offline-first Android app for finding bus timetables, viewing routes and community feedback, and submitting timetable suggestions and other contributions. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Sanchari (Android application ID: com.sanchari.bus).

Sanchari is operated by Jis N George ("we", "us", or "our").

Sanchari is an open-source app. Its Android source code and app releases are available at github.com/jisngeorge/SanchariApp. Published timetable and community database updates are available at github.com/jisngeorge/SanchariVersions. Making the app source and database updates public does not make private submission records or contact details public.

1. Privacy at a glance

  • Timetable and bus-name searches run against databases on your device. Search terms and recent service views are not included in contribution uploads or update requests.
  • Device location is optional and used on your device to find nearby stops. Sanchari does not send your device fix or manually entered coordinates to its contribution backend.
  • Contributions are submitted through Google Apps Script and stored in Google Sheets for timetable and community-data processing. Every contribution includes a randomly generated contributor identifier, even when you do not provide a name or contact details.
  • The developer is the only person who receives and reviews private submission JSON. Raw submissions and private contact records are not shared with other people or published in the app's repositories. Google processes uploads as the hosting and spreadsheet provider.
  • Name, email address, phone number and place are optional. Only details you enter for that contribution are attached; previously saved contacts are not automatically attached. Optional contact/profile details are used only to contact you for clarifications and replies to your queries. A name can also appear on a public comment if you explicitly choose to display it.
  • The current Android app has no advertising, advertising-ID collection, analytics SDK or crash-reporting SDK. Hosting providers may process technical information when you connect to their services.

2. Information handled by Sanchari

Information kept on your device
The app stores downloaded timetable and community databases, a local contributor identifier, any saved profile/contact details, the latest five distinct services viewed, successful schedule-submission history, unfinished timetable drafts, pending contributions and app preferences. Preferences include update settings and version-check information. Drafts may be saved automatically when you leave or background the editor. These records support offline access, resuming edits and retrying submissions; ordinary search and recent-view records are not uploaded by the app.
Contributor identifier
Sanchari creates a random UUID for the local profile and includes it in every submitted contribution. It is not an advertising ID, IMEI, phone number or hardware identifier. It allows contributions from the same local profile to be associated. Processing uses this identifier to maintain contribution/review records and the latest rating or running-status report for a contributor and service. Ratings can be replaced without counting the same contributor as an additional rater. It is pseudonymous, not a guarantee of anonymity: contributions can be linked to contact details you provide and to their content.
Optional personal details
You may enter your name, email address, phone number and place on the contribution confirmation screen. We collect these details only if you supply them voluntarily, and use them only to contact you for clarifications and replies to your queries, subject to the separate public-name choice for comments described below. Nonblank details entered there are saved locally and sent with that contribution. Blank fields are omitted from the upload, but do not erase previously saved local details or earlier submissions. The app does not read your device's address book.
Contribution content
When you submit, we receive your contributor identifier, a submission timestamp, any optional details entered for that submission, and the contribution itself. This can include bus and service identifiers, service names and types, route stops and timetable times, edit notes, ratings for punctuality, driving and behaviour, comments, a public-name display choice, running-status suggestions, or messages to the administrator. Please avoid putting unnecessary personal information or information about other people into free-text fields.
Location and manual coordinates
With your permission, Sanchari accesses precise or approximate device location through Android's GPS/network providers only when you request current-location assistance. It compares that location with stored stop coordinates on your device. There is no background location tracking. Active acquisition stops when you leave the activity; device fixes are discarded when the popup closes or the app backgrounds. Manual coordinate text and radius may survive screen recreation in temporary Android UI state. Sanchari does not keep a persistent coordinate history, include those coordinates in contribution payloads, or write them to its logs.
Network and hosting information
The app connects to GitHub Pages and configured download hosts to check for and download updates, and to Google Apps Script to submit contributions. Those services receive your IP address and technical request information needed to deliver the connection; they may retain service logs under their own policies. Normal update requests do not attach your contributor UUID, saved contacts, search terms or device location.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide offline timetable searches and route displays, find nearby stops at your request, maintain local drafts and history, deliver database/app updates, receive and review community contributions, and respond to messages or questions using details you choose to provide. Uploading a contribution does not immediately change the timetable or community database; publication follows a separate review and update process.

The processing workflow uses contribution content and contributor identifiers to review suggestions, avoid applying the same submission repeatedly, maintain review/audit records and contributor activity or reputation records, and update the timetable and community databases. Private contributor records may retain details previously supplied; omitting them in a later submission does not erase earlier records.

Your optional email address, phone number, place and privately supplied name are not used for advertising or marketing. Their use is limited to contacting you for clarifications and replies to your queries. If you explicitly choose to display your name on a comment, that name is also used for the public attribution you requested.

We do not sell personal information or use private submissions for advertising, marketing or tracking across other apps or websites.

4. Sharing and public contributions

Submission processing

The app's submission form sends information to a Google Apps Script endpoint, which records contributions in Google Sheets. Google provides the hosting and spreadsheet infrastructure. Contributions can then be exported and imported into a separate, non-public administrator processing application for validation, review and database updates.

The processor keeps submission content, contributor identifiers, supplied personal details and processing decisions in private records. Imported submissions, exports, archives and backups can also contain these details. Messages to the administrator are handled in a private inbox, rather than published as community comments. The developer, Jis N George, is the only person receiving and reviewing the raw submission JSON and accessing these private records on our side. We do not share these records with other reviewers, organizations or members of the public.

Community publication

Accepted timetable information and community feedback may be distributed in public database snapshots used by Sanchari. A comment's public-name choice is included in its submission. If you do not supply a name, the app sets that choice to hidden and previews the name as "Anonymous". Hiding a public name does not remove the private contributor identifier or optional contacts sent to the administrator. Free-text content may itself identify you.

Public timetable data includes bus/service information, stop names and coordinates, ordered route stops and scheduled times. Public community data includes aggregate rating averages and rating counts, and approved comments with their service reference, date and display name. When a comment is accepted, the processor uses "Anonymous" if you hide your name or supply no name; it does not substitute a saved contact name or contributor UUID. Individual contributor ratings and running-status reports are kept in private processing records; approved running-status changes may update the public service's status.

Contributor UUIDs, email addresses, phone numbers, profile place details, private submission/review records and messages to the administrator are not part of the public database publication described here. A name you choose to display on an approved comment is public. Personal information you put into a public comment's text can also become public.

Only timetable and community database snapshots are published as database updates. Private contributor and processing databases, raw submission JSON, spreadsheet exports, private archives and backups are not published. Public contribution content is separate from these private source records.

Maps and other user-initiated external actions

If you choose to show a stop or open driving directions, Sanchari opens Google Maps or a browser with the stored coordinates of the selected stop or route endpoints. These are bus-stop coordinates, not the device fix used for nearby-stop lookup. The receiving app or website may process those coordinates, your IP address and other information under its own privacy policy. Sanchari does not control any additional location access you allow in that external app. Sharing the app's download link similarly opens the sharing app you select.

Other disclosures

Apart from Google processing submissions to provide the hosting and spreadsheet service, and the public contribution content described above, we do not voluntarily disclose private submission data to anyone other than the developer. We may disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal order, limited to what is legally required.

Relevant provider policies: Google Privacy Policy and GitHub General Privacy Statement. These also apply to interactions with their services as described by those providers. Opening this policy on GitHub Pages sends a web request to GitHub.

5. Permissions and your choices

You can search installed timetables without granting location permission, submitting contributions or providing personal details. You can deny or revoke location permission in Android Settings, use approximate location, enter coordinates manually, or choose stops by name instead.

Internet access supports submissions and updates. Database auto-update is enabled by default and can be turned off through the app's Auto-update setting. Automatic version checks can still occur; disabling auto-update changes whether newer databases are downloaded without a prompt. Downloads can use mobile data. Installing an APK update requires your confirmation and any applicable Android installation permission.

Providing personal details is optional for every contribution. To stop attaching a detail to new contributions, leave that field blank. This does not change contributions already sent or queued. Failed uploads can be saved on your device and retried automatically on a later app launch, using the original content, identifier, timestamp and contact choices.

6. Retention and security

Local profile information, preferences and schedule-submission history remain until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Recent views are limited to five distinct services. Drafts remain until explicitly discarded or removed after a contribution is successfully uploaded or successfully saved for retry. Pending upload files remain until the app reports a successful upload or local app data is removed. Removing a draft does not withdraw a queued contribution.

Processing or rejecting a contribution does not by itself erase its original submission or personal details. Private processing records, imported files, archives and backups may retain those details separately from the Google Sheet. Local device-data deletion does not remove these copies.

We retain private submissions and optional contact details until the contributor requests deletion. This includes received or rejected contributions, contributor identifiers, messages, review/audit and contributor activity records, and identifiable copies in exports, imported files, archives and backups under the developer's control. There is no fixed automatic expiry period for these private records.

When handling a verified deletion request, we remove the identifiable records and copies we control. Public timetable and community data remain available to support the app, subject to requests concerning personal information described below. Technical service logs and provider-managed copies outside our control are subject to Google's or GitHub's policies; this policy does not promise deletion beyond the controls those services provide.

Local records are kept in Android app-private storage; this is not a claim of additional app-level encryption. The app disables Android cloud backup through its manifest. Device-to-device transfer behavior can depend on Android and the device manufacturer. Contributions are transmitted to the configured Google Apps Script service over HTTPS.

The processing application separates public timetable/community data from private contributor and review records. Contact details are hidden in ordinary review views unless the operator explicitly requests them. This interface safeguard is not a claim that the underlying private files are encrypted or protected from everyone with access to the operator's computer.

The developer keeps private submission records separate from the public source-code repositories, app releases and database updates. Access on our side is limited to the developer. We do not claim additional application-level encryption of spreadsheet exports or local processor files. Google handles security of its hosting infrastructure under its service policies.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Deletion requests and privacy rights

Sanchari does not require registration or provide an online sign-in account. It maintains a local contributor profile and identifier. To remove data held on your device, use Android Settings > Apps > Sanchari > Storage > Clear storage (wording varies by device), or uninstall Sanchari. This removes local drafts and queued contributions as well as local contacts and history, but does not delete contributions already received by the backend. Discarding a draft removes only that draft.

To request access, correction or deletion of submitted personal information, contact jisngeorge@gmail.com and use the subject "Sanchari privacy request". Describe the relevant contribution, approximate submission date, and any contact detail you originally supplied. Do not send identity documents or unrelated sensitive information unless a necessary, proportionate verification process is explained.

Because contributions can be made without contact details, identifying a particular contributor's records may require additional information. If you provided no contact details, describe the bus/service, approximate date and content of your submission so the developer can try to locate it. You are not required to obtain a contributor UUID from the app. We may request proportionate additional information to verify that a request concerns your records; we cannot delete another person's information based on an unverified claim.

The developer reviews requests and responds without undue delay, within any timeframe required by applicable law. Once the relevant records are identified and the request is verified, deletion covers personal information in the Google Sheet and any accessible revision history, private contributor/processing records, imported files, exports, archives and identifiable backup copies under the developer's control. Personal information in public comments is removed from future publications where applicable. We will explain any inability to identify a record, provider-controlled limitation or specific legal requirement preventing deletion, rather than represent the request as completed.

Already downloaded or independently copied public timetable/community snapshots may remain on other people's devices; removal from future publications cannot recall every existing copy. Deleting local app data can create a new contributor identifier on later use but does not unlink or erase earlier backend records.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. Contact the developer using the privacy contact below to exercise applicable rights or raise a privacy grievance. We process voluntary contributions to provide the requested submission and review service, and use optional contact details for the follow-up you choose to enable. Where processing requires consent under applicable law, you may withdraw that consent by contacting us; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.
